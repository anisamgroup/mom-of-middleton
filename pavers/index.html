Paver questions

Pavers in The Villages: your questions

Where do you install pavers?

Anywhere in The Villages and Middleton, FL. That's a wider area than our exterior cleaning, which focuses on Middleton and the Eastport villages.

What can you install?

Paver driveways, patios, walkways and front entries, and pool decks with matching coping for the pool edge.

How much do new pavers cost?

Every project is different, so Marco comes to your home, talks through what you want and measures before giving you a price. The estimate is free. Call or text 352-808-2082.

Is sealing included?

Yes. We come back to seal your new pavers once they've had time to settle. Waiting lets any natural white haze from new pavers come out first, so it isn't sealed in.

Which pavers do you use for driveways?

Driveways get 60 mm pavers, made to hold cars and golf carts. We also carry 30 mm pavers, in 6 shapes and sizes and 15 blended colors.

Can I see the colors in person?

Yes. Colors on a screen are a guide only, so ask to see a real sample before you choose. Tell us the color code (for example E3) when you call.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Your pavers are installed by licensed, insured paver professionals.

Can MOM keep my pavers clean afterward?

Yes. Add your new pavers to MOM's regular cleaning or the Protection Plan, and we'll reseal them when it's time.
