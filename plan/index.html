MOM Home Investment Protection Plan · 12-Month Service Agreement

This Service Agreement (“Agreement”) is entered into between MOM of Middleton (“MOM,” “we,” “us”) and the customer identified below (“Customer,” “you”), and governs enrollment in the MOM Home Investment Protection Plan (the “Plan”).

2. Program & Pricing

MOM will first perform a complete initial house wash and Complete Exterior Package cleaning (driveway, walkway, entry, gutters, and back patio/lanai) at the applicable Full-Service Regular Price. Beginning the following service cycle, Customer enrolls in the Plan level selected below at the corresponding monthly rate.

Plan Level Selected:

  • Service is automatically performed at the frequency of the Plan level selected above (Standard: every 4 months, 3 visits per year; Enhanced: every 3 months, 4 visits per year; Premium: every 2 months, 6 visits per year). Every visit includes the Complete Exterior Package (driveway, walkway, entry, exterior gutters, back patio/lanai) at every visit.

  • No additional charge applies to Plan visits for a back patio/lanai that extends beyond the roofline.
  • Payment is due monthly via automatic payment on the method provided by Customer at enrollment.

3. Term & Automatic Renewal

This Agreement begins on the Effective Date below and continues for an initial term of twelve (12) consecutive months (the “Initial Term”). Upon completion of the Initial Term, this Agreement automatically renews for an additional twelve (12) month term (each, a “Renewal Term”) under the same terms, unless either party provides written notice of cancellation at least thirty (30) days before the Initial Term or then-current Renewal Term ends.

4. Early Cancellation

Customer agrees to remain enrolled in the Plan for the full 12-month term then in effect. Because MOM prices this program below its standard Full-Service rate on the expectation of a full year of service, early cancellation before completion of the then-current 12-month term is permitted only in the following circumstances:

  • Documented hardship (e.g., divorce, death, medical hardship, or similar significant life circumstance), approved in writing at MOM of Middleton’s sole discretion on a case-by-case basis.
  • Mutual written agreement between Customer and MOM.

The sale or transfer of the property is not, by itself, a qualifying reason for early cancellation; in that circumstance, Section 5 (Transfer on Sale) applies instead.

If Customer cancels for any other reason before completing the then-current 12-month term, Customer agrees to pay an Early Termination Fee equal to the difference between the Full-Service Regular Price for each completed service visit and the discounted Plan rate actually paid for those visits, reflecting the savings already received under the program. This fee will be invoiced upon cancellation.

5. Transfer on Sale

If Customer sells or otherwise transfers ownership of the property during the Initial Term, Customer may assign the remainder of this Agreement to the new owner, subject to MOM’s approval (not to be unreasonably withheld). MOM will honor the remaining service schedule and rate for the new owner through the end of the Initial Term. Customer must notify MOM in writing at least fourteen (14) days before the closing/transfer date to arrange the assignment.

6. What’s Included

Each maintenance visit includes house wash and the Complete Exterior Package: driveway, walkway, entry, exterior gutters, and back patio/lanai. This includes cleaning of the exterior surface of the gutters only.

The following separate services are not included and are billed separately if requested:

  • Interior gutter flushing / water flow-through cleaning
  • Fence cleaning
  • Paver sealing

7. Access & Scheduling

MOM will contact Customer to schedule each service visit within the applicable service window for the selected Plan level. Customer agrees to provide reasonable access to the property and to secure pets, vehicles, and outdoor items as needed. If MOM is unable to access the property after reasonable attempts to schedule, that visit may be forfeited, though monthly billing continues per the payment schedule in Section 2. MOM may, at its discretion, offer to reschedule a forfeited visit if Customer provides access before the next scheduled service window.

8. Payment Terms

Monthly payments are due on the same calendar day each month via the payment method Customer provides. A failed or declined payment may result in a late fee and/or suspension of scheduled service until the account is brought current. Two or more consecutive missed payments may be treated as a cancellation subject to Section 4.

9. General Terms

  • This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Florida.
  • This Agreement, including any signed proposal or invoice referencing it, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties regarding the Plan.
  • If any provision of this Agreement is found unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in full effect.

Acknowledgment

By signing below, Customer acknowledges having read and agreed to the terms of this Agreement, including the 12-month commitment and Early Cancellation terms described in Section 4.
